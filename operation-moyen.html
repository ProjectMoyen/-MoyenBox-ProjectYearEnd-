House rules Moyen never breaks

  • No hotel logo or brand on posters, stickers, or the site unless Purchasing says yes in writing. Moyen is the brand.
  • No real PO in a screenshot, video, or group chat. Hotel contacts are on them.
  • Promise only what the site does today. If a vendor asks for a feature, write it on the complaints list.
  • Moyen stays in every version. He is the creator's credit.
